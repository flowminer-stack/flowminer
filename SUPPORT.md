# Getting help with FlowMiner

FlowMiner is open-source (MIT) and self-hostable. Here is where to get help —
and what your options are if you need a guarantee behind it.

## Community support (free)

- **Questions, ideas, "how do I…?"** → [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/flowminer/flowminer/discussions)
- **Bugs and feature requests** → [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/flowminer/flowminer/issues)
  (use the bug-report / feature-request templates)
- **Docs** → the [`README`](./README.md), [`docs/`](./docs/), and
  [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md)
- **Security vulnerabilities** → do **not** open a public issue; follow
  [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)

Community support is best-effort, provided by maintainers and other users.
Please search existing issues/discussions before posting, and include your
deployment details (version, OS, Docker setup) and steps to reproduce.

## Commercial support & managed hosting (optional)

Unlike the open-core competitors, **the whole product is the open product** —
there is no paywalled "enterprise edition" holding the useful features hostage.
But if your organization needs a name to call when something breaks in
production, the following are available as optional, separately-contracted
services:

- **Priority support with an SLA** — guaranteed response times.
- **Managed hosting** — we run a hardened FlowMiner instance for you.
- **Deployment, data-extraction, and onboarding assistance** — including
  air-gapped / on-prem and ERP (SAP/Oracle/etc.) event-log extraction.
- **Custom development & prioritized features.**

For any of these, email **hello@flowminer.io**.

> Choosing commercial support never changes the license of the software you run
> or locks up your data — you keep the MIT-licensed product and full export of
> your projects either way.

## Governance & maintainership

FlowMiner is maintained openly on GitHub. Roadmap planning lives in
[`docs/roadmap/`](./docs/roadmap/). Maintainers triage issues and review PRs;
see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for how decisions are made and how to
get involved.
